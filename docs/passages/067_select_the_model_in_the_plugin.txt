After [[configuring OpenCode->Configure OpenCode for Ollama]], pick your local model in the plugin:

1.  Click the gear icon (or press `Tab` in the input area) to open the chat options panel.
2.  Pick the **agent** you want to use (e.g. `build` or `plan`).
3.  Pick your **model** from the model dropdown — the local models configured above will appear there, listed as their provider/model names.
4.  Choose a suitable **Thinking Level** if the model supports one.

Every query and indexing task now runs entirely on your machine — no data leaves your computer. See [[Offline/Private Coding (Local LLMs)]] for privacy notes and [[Picking an Agent, Model and Thinking Level]] for more on the selectors.
