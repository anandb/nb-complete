The chat panel is packed with features to help you get the most out of the assistant.

#### Stopping a turn

While a turn is running, the **Go** button becomes **Stop**. Pressing it sends the harness a `session/cancel` notification, keeps the partial response on screen, and restores the input and toolbar immediately rather than waiting for the harness to acknowledge. Stop only ends the current turn — it does not close the session or kill the harness process; use **Restart Harness** for that. Stop also resets the panel when the toolbar was left busy without an active turn.
