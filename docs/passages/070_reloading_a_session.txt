The **Reload** button (reload icon) on the toolbar re-fetches the current session from the OpenCode server. Your messages are always stored on the server, so reloading never loses anything — it just refreshes the chat view to match the server state.

You'll want to reload when:

- you ran <code>/compact</code> and want the summarised history displayed
- older messages were trimmed from the view (click **Show All Messages** first, then **Reload** to bring them all back)
- the view seems out of sync with the server, for example after a server restart or a session reload from the dropdown
