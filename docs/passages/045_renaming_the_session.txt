To keep your conversations organised, you can rename any session. Click the **Pencil icon** in the chat UI or right-click a session in the session dropdown to give it a custom name. Alternatively, type the `/title` slash command to let the AI automatically generate and apply a suitable title based on the conversation context. These custom names are stored locally by the plugin, not in the OpenCode DB.
