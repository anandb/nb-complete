When you start a new session, a slide-in confirmation bar appears above the input area: *"Select the agent, model and level below, then click Continue."* The agent, model, and thinking-level dropdowns stay locked until you confirm your selection, so the session starts with the configuration you intend. Clicking **Continue** unlocks the dropdowns and starts the session.
