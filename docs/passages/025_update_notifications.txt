The plugin checks for new releases in the background at randomised 16–24 hour intervals. When an update is available, a dialog offers three choices:

*   **Download Now** — open the release to download and install
*   **Remind me Later**
*   **Skip this Version**

Disable the check under `Options > BeanBot > Updates > Check for Updates`.

You can also check on demand: click the **?** (help) button in the toolbar and choose **Check for Updates**. The outcome is shown on the status line (for example *You are up to date* or *An update is available*).
