Click the **Currency/Token Stats** icon on the toolbar to open the token and cost panel. It summarises every harness from data captured locally in the IDE: session, message and active-day counts, plus total cost, per-day averages, per-session token averages and median, and the summed **Input**, **Output** and **Cache Read** figures. Pick a day window and filter to the current project or all projects, then press **Refresh**. Hover over the status label (which normally says "Ready") to view the context window fill level. This helps you decide when to run `/compact` to summarise the conversation and free up context.

**Note:** These figures are approximate. They are assembled from the usage data each harness reports, and harnesses report it differently — some include the cached read in their input count, and subscription-backed or free models often report no cost at all, so **Total Cost** understates their usage. Expect minor discrepancies: treat the panel as a close estimate of your usage, not an exact bill, and do not be surprised if it differs slightly from your provider's own dashboard.
