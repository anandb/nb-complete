Your message history is stored by the agent, not by the IDE. While the local chat view hides older messages to conserve IDE memory, clicking **Show All Messages** stops the trimming, and **Reload** asks the agent to replay the session — every message the agent still replays comes back. Newer agents compact their context once a session grows large, and the replay then starts at that compaction point: the compacted-away turns stay in the agent's own history log but are no longer sent to the IDE, so a reload cannot show them. Search your past input history with `Ctrl + R`. If you want to keep just one or two messages visible (e.g. a plan or to-do list), hover over the response bubble and click the pin icon; pinned messages stay in the chat panel instead of being cleared over time.
