If your conversation becomes very long and approaches its context token limit, ask the harness to compact it. Enter `/compact` in the chat input, or right-click the [[Context Usage Meter]] in the assistant's bottom status row and choose **Compact**. The command asks the harness to summarise the conversation and reduce the context it uses; the exact behavior depends on the harness. Check the meter's tooltip for used and total tokens, and see [[Check token usage]] for historical usage reports.
