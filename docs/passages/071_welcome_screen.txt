When the assistant panel is opened with no session selected, you'll see the welcome screen instead of a chat thread. It shows a **Start New Chat** button plus a list of your existing sessions, each with the project folder it belongs to shown underneath the title.

Click any session to open it, or **Start New Chat** to begin a fresh conversation. Sessions you have archived (hidden) are only listed when hidden sessions are visible — use the eye icon on the toolbar to toggle them.
