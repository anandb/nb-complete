If the server process dies or its pipes break mid-conversation, the plugin handles it automatically:

1. any stale server process is killed and its connections closed
2. a notification appears: **Harness Disconnected — Attempting to reconnect...**
3. the server is restarted automatically with a short backoff (about 3, 6, then 9 seconds)

The plugin gives up after **3 failed restarts within 5 minutes** and shows a "server crashed" status message. In that case check your installation, then click **Restart Harness** to try again. Your sessions are not lost during a reconnect — they are stored on the server and simply reloaded.
